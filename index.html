Welcome

I am Vrushtee Gaikwad. 19, Mumbai.

I am hopping through fun and funny moments in life. I love to make things happen with surprises.

I have been making robots since a year now, it allows me to do dreamy things.

I want to be in awe of humanity's creations, and create the ones that make me awe. I have been a Bharatanatyam dancer since the age of 5, one of the things that keeps me happy, grounded and expressive.

Creating is about making asymmetric pieces spring into action. Things that do not resonate, do not vibrate with each other. They just coalesce to form an object, a machine, or a device of great help. I love to learn and to make robots learn.
